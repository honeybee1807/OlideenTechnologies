Let's Talk

The pitch, honestly

I don't build websites.
I build the tool that brings you work.

Most small businesses don't need a fancier site — they need one that shows up on Google, loads fast on a bad signal, and makes it genuinely easy for someone to WhatsApp, call, or book.

That's what I do. I'm one person, based in Estcourt. I answer my own messages. I write the code myself. If it's not working, I'm the one who fixes it — usually the same day.

Built around your goals

I start by asking what success looks like for your business in six months. Then I build backwards from that. If a feature doesn't serve that goal, we skip it.

Transparent pricing, up front

You get a written quote with a fixed scope. No hourly surprises. No "but that's out of scope." If something genuinely changes, we agree on the new number before I touch the keyboard.

Always reachable — WhatsApp first

Email works, but most clients just message me. I answer within a few hours during the working day, same day on weekends. No ticketing system.
