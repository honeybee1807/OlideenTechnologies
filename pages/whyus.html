Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a website actually cost?

For a solid 3–5 page brochure site with WhatsApp + contact form, SEO setup and hosting, expect mid-single-digit thousands of rand. A web app with logins and a dashboard is higher. I always give you a written fixed quote before you commit, with no hourly billing surprises.

What if I don't know what I want?

That's the most common starting point. Book the 30-minute call. Tell me what your business does and what's not working. I'll ask questions and write up a scope document you can take home and think about.

Do I own the code and files when we're done?

Yes, 100%. On launch you get the domain in your name, the code in a repo you can keep, and a handover document.

Are you going to disappear after launch?

No, that's a freelancer stereotype, and fair to worry about. I include 30 days of tweaks and bug-fixes after launch, and offer ongoing monthly support from then on if you want it. This is a real business, not a side hustle.

Can you match what a specific agency quoted me?

Usually yes. Send me the quote, I'll tell you honestly what's worth doing, what's padding, and what I'd build differently.

Do you only work with KZN businesses?

No. KZN is home, but I work with clients across South Africa. Everything is handled over WhatsApp and Microsoft Teams Meetings, so location doesn't actually matter.

How fast can you start?

Usually within 1–2 weeks of signing. I keep my pipeline intentionally short so I can give each project my full attention. If a slot is tight, I'll tell you.
