Common questions

FAQs

What does a website typically cost?

A 3–5 page brochure site with WhatsApp, a contact form, SEO and hosting usually lands in the mid-single-digit thousands of rand. Web apps with logins and dashboards cost more. Either way, you get a fixed written price before you commit. No hourly billing, no hidden extras.

I’m not sure what I need. Is that okay?

Completely normal. Book a 30-minute call, tell us about your business, and we’ll respond with a written scope you can take away and think about.

Do I own the code and assets?

Yes, completely. At launch you get the domain in your name, the code in a repo you own, and a handover document.

Will you still be around after launch?

Yes. Every project comes with 30 days of free post-launch tweaks and fixes. Monthly retainers are available for ongoing support.

Can you match a quote I’ve already got?

Usually, yes. Send it to us and we’ll tell you honestly what’s worth keeping, what’s padded, and what we’d do differently.

Do you only work with KZN clients?

No. KZN is home, but we work with clients all over South Africa. WhatsApp and Teams mean location barely matters.

How soon can we start?

Usually 1–2 weeks after sign-off. We keep our pipeline small so every project gets real attention. If we’re full, we’ll tell you straight away.
