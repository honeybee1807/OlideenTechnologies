Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026 — Olideen Technologies is committed to safeguarding your personal information.

1. Who We Are

Olideen Technologies is a web and software development business based in KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa. We provide websites, web applications, mobile applications, and related digital solutions for individuals and businesses.

Contact: [email protected]  |  +27 73 307 5218

2. Information We Collect

We collect only personal information that you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Your name and contact details (email address and telephone number)
  • Business name and project requirements submitted through our contact form
  • Correspondence history (WhatsApp and email)

We do not collect sensitive personal information such as identity numbers, financial account details, or health information.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and prepare quotations
  • Deliver the services for which you have engaged us
  • Provide project updates and correspondence related to your work
  • Improve our services in response to feedback

We will never sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. POPIA Compliance

We process personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), Act 4 of 2013 of South Africa. You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information, where applicable
  • Object to the processing of your information

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at [email protected].

5. Third-Party Services

Our contact form is processed through EmailJS. Submissions are therefore subject to the EmailJS privacy policy. This service is used solely to receive your enquiry.

Our website incorporates typefaces from Google Fonts and icons from Font Awesome, both of which may collect basic usage data in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect your personal information. Correspondence is conducted via encrypted channels wherever possible (email and WhatsApp). We do not store payment information.

7. Cookies

This website does not currently use tracking cookies or third-party analytics services. Basic browser session data may be stored locally and is not transmitted to us.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a revised date. Continued use of our website constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
