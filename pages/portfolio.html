Estcourt Waste Collectors is a family-owned business that has been keeping KwaZulu-Natal cleaner for over 25 years. They handle household and commercial waste collection, responsible recycling across paper, plastics, metals and glass, and even sell affordable salvaged goods to the community. Despite a 4.9-star reputation built on 653 community reviews, they had no web presence at all.

The brief was simple: build a fast, mobile-first site that works on a slow 3G signal, turns visitors into WhatsApp bookings, and gives the business the credibility it has long deserved. No missed collections, no forms to wrestle with — just a tap and a chat.
