Rank Pharmacy

An independent community pharmacy in Estcourt, KZN — SAPC-compliant, SEO-optimised and built from scratch with hand-coded HTML, CSS and JS. No templates. No WordPress. A digital presence that matches the professionalism of a pharmacist who’s served the same families for decades.

The Brief — Rank Pharmacy had no website. The pharmacist, Goolam Rassool Haroon Olideen, had been serving the Estcourt community for decades with a 5-star reputation but zero digital presence. The brief was a professional, informational site: no online store, no payment gateway — just a trusted front door that turns a visit into a WhatsApp or a phone call.

The Challenge — A pharmacy website is one of the most regulated categories in South African web design. The SAPC Good Pharmacy Practice Manual, the Medicines Act and the SAPC Code of Conduct all apply. That meant no testimonials, no comparative pricing, no therapeutic claims for supplements, a full Privacy Policy (POPIA), a complaints procedure with SAPC referral details, and the responsible pharmacist’s name and registration number displayed on the homepage — not just the About page. Eleven compliance issues were identified and resolved before the site went live.

The Build — Six pages of hand-coded HTML, CSS and vanilla JS — no frameworks, no WordPress, no templates. A Three.js animated pills background. Full SEO on every page including JSON-LD Pharmacy schema, Open Graph, canonical tags and a sitemap. Professional email routing via Cloudflare (info@rankpharmacy.co.za), contact form wired through EmailJS, and DNS managed entirely through the developer’s Cloudflare account. Deployed on GitHub Pages with Cloudflare DNS and automatic SSL.

The Result — A fully live, SAPC-compliant, SEO-optimised pharmacy website indexed by Google within days of launch — with the business now appearing in Google’s local map pack for Estcourt pharmacy searches.

Estcourt Waste Collectors is a family-run business that’s served KwaZulu-Natal for over 25 years. They do household and commercial waste collection, recycling across paper, plastic, metal and glass, and run a community salvage store.
