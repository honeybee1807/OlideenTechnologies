Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026 — Olideen Technologies is committed to protecting your personal information.

1. Who We Are

Olideen Technologies is a web and software development business based in Estcourt, KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa. We build websites, web applications, mobile apps, and digital solutions for individuals and businesses.

Contact: olideentech@gmail.com  |  +27 73 307 5218

2. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us, including:

  • Your name and contact details (email, phone number)
  • Business name and project requirements shared via our contact form
  • Communication history (WhatsApp, email)

We do not collect sensitive personal information such as ID numbers, financial account details, or health information.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and provide quotations
  • Deliver the services you have engaged us for
  • Send project updates and communication related to your work
  • Improve our services based on feedback

We will never sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. POPIA Compliance

We process personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), Act 4 of 2013 of South Africa. You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information where applicable
  • Object to the processing of your information

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at olideentech@gmail.com.

5. Third-Party Services

Our contact form uses Formspree to process submissions. By submitting the form, your information is also subject to Formspree's privacy policy. We use this solely to receive your enquiry.

Our website uses fonts from Google Fonts and icons from Font Awesome, both of which may collect basic usage data per their respective privacy policies.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect your personal information. Communications are conducted via encrypted channels where possible (email, WhatsApp). We do not store payment information.

7. Cookies

This website does not currently use tracking cookies or analytics services. Basic browser functionality may store session data locally, which is never transmitted to us.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any significant changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of our website constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
